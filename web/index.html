How it works

Two open models, one honest answer.

01

You describe your spot

Latitude, elevation, and your late-winter temperatures. A preset fills these from real climate norms, or type your own.

02

TabPFN-v2 forecasts

An open-weight tabular foundation model predicts your last-frost day-of-year in one CPU pass, with a 10–90% range. No training, no cloud.

03

Gemma writes the plan

A local open-weight Gemma 3 model turns that date into plain-language planting guidance for your exact crop.

Why open innovation matters

The open pieces are the whole point.

Works with no signal. Both models run on your machine. On a hillside allotment with one bar of signal, a closed API is useless. FrostWise still answers.

Your location stays yours. Where you garden never leaves your laptop. No server, no account, nothing logged to someone else's cloud.

Costs nothing to run. Open weights, zero per-request fees. Run it a thousand times for a community garden and the bill is still $0.

Swap the brain. Point it at a bigger Gemma, or retrain TabPFN on your own weather station. You own the stack, not a vendor.
